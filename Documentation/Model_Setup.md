# COMSOL Model Setup

## 1. Model Overview

A three-dimensional thermoelectric module model was developed using COMSOL Multiphysics to investigate the coupled thermal and electrical behavior of the system.

The model consists of thermoelectric legs, electrical conductors, and ceramic insulating regions.

The thermoelectric material used in the model is Bi₂Te₂.₇₀Se₀.₃₀, while copper and alumina are used for the conducting and insulating regions respectively.

---

## 2. Geometry

The model is developed as a 3D thermoelectric module.

The main regions of the geometry are:

- Thermoelectric legs
- Copper conducting regions
- Al₂O₃ ceramic/insulating regions

The geometry is designed to allow the investigation of the influence of the thermoelectric leg geometry on module performance.

The H/L ratio is used as the main geometric parameter in the parametric study.

Where:

- **H** = Thermoelectric leg height
- **L** = Characteristic thermoelectric leg length

---

## 3. Materials

The following materials are used in the model:

### Thermoelectric Material

**Bi₂Te₂.₇₀Se₀.₃₀**

The thermoelectric material is responsible for the conversion between thermal and electrical energy through thermoelectric effects.

Important material properties include:

- Electrical conductivity
- Thermal conductivity
- Seebeck coefficient

### Electrical Conductor

**Copper (Cu)**

Copper is used as the electrically conducting region connecting the thermoelectric elements.

### Ceramic Insulator

**Al₂O₃**

Al₂O₃ is used as the ceramic/insulating material of the thermoelectric module.

---

## 4. Physics Interfaces

The following COMSOL physics interfaces are used:

### Heat Transfer in Solids

This physics interface is used to model heat conduction and temperature distribution throughout the solid domains.

### Electric Currents

This physics interface is used to calculate:

- Electric potential
- Current density
- Electrical response

### Thermoelectric Effect

The thermoelectric coupling accounts for the interaction between the thermal and electrical fields.

The thermoelectric behavior includes effects such as the Seebeck effect and the interaction between temperature gradients and electrical potential.

---

## 5. Study

A **Stationary Study** is used to obtain the steady-state thermal and electrical solution.

The stationary formulation is used to investigate the final temperature and electrical distributions after the system reaches steady conditions.

---

## 6. Model Parameters

Important parameters used in the simulation include:

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Reference Temperature | 293.15 K |
| Load Resistance | 0.42 Ω |
| Input Voltage | 7.125 V |
| Study Type | Stationary |
| Main Parametric Variable | H/L |

The H/L ratio is varied during the parametric study to investigate its influence on thermoelectric performance.

---

## 7. Boundary Conditions

Thermal and electrical boundary conditions are applied to the appropriate surfaces of the thermoelectric module.

The thermal boundary conditions establish the temperature gradient required for thermoelectric operation.

Electrical boundary conditions are applied to define the electrical circuit and load conditions.

The exact boundary conditions are defined within the COMSOL model according to the selected simulation configuration.

---

## 8. Reference Temperature

The reference temperature used in the model is:

**293.15 K**

This temperature is used as the reference condition for the thermoelectric model.

---

## 9. Load Condition

A load resistance of:

**0.42 Ω**

is used in the current simulation.

The load condition is used to evaluate the electrical power generated by the thermoelectric module.

---
